POLICY TYPE: INDIVIDUAL_HEALTH

CLAUSE: ROOM RENT LIMIT

The eligible room rent is limited to 1 percent of the Sum Insured per day,
subject to the maximum room category permitted under the policy.

If the insured selects a room exceeding the eligible room rent limit,
proportionate deductions may apply to eligible associated expenses
such as nursing charges and professional fees, where applicable
under the policy terms.

CLAUSE: WAITING PERIOD

Specific listed diseases are subject to a waiting period of 24 months
from the policy commencement date, unless specifically waived or
modified by the policy schedule.

CLAUSE: NON-PAYABLE ITEMS

Items such as registration charges, admission charges, administrative
charges, food charges, toiletries and certain consumables may be
non-payable according to the applicable policy terms and standard
exclusion rules.

CLAUSE: SUM INSURED

The maximum amount payable under the policy cannot exceed the
available Sum Insured, including applicable accumulated or exhausted
limits.

CLAUSE: MEDICAL NECESSITY

Hospitalization and treatment must be medically necessary and
consistent with the diagnosed medical condition and applicable
policy terms.
